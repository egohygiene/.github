## Plain-language architecture

The organization combines public knowledge/products with separately owned policy, reusable AI methodology, local environment/tooling, evidence collection, normalization and publication.

Hygiene owns canonical admission/policy; Aether methodology; Reflector campaign transport; Observatory read models; Relay reusable execution/rendering; Pace adoption; repositories their own source and publication.

Explicit source revisions and public admission feed typed evidence, owner-normalized models and repository/organization review surfaces. Media engines remain independently composed through Flow; environment intent and shell behavior remain separated.

Keeping authored intent, execution, observation, rendering and human governance separate supports replaceability and prevents a green check or source merge becoming architecture approval.
